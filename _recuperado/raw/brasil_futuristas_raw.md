## About Fluxonomia 4D

The term, idealized by futurist Lala Deheinzelin, involves four types of economy: Creative, Collaborative, Shared, and Multivalue. They are in a transition of models of living, working, and managing.

The four economies are:

1. Creative Economy: flow of "raw materials" that are abundant, not consumed but multiplied with use, such as culture, knowledge, creativity, and community experiences

2. Sharing Economy: flow of infrastructure in a more accessible and sustainable way through sharing of spaces, equipment, and materials

3. Collaborative Economy: flow of initiatives connected and converging potential through distributed management and network processes

4. Multivalue Economy: flow of resources and results of all kinds, not just monetary but also cultural, environmental, and social
