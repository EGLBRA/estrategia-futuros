# WFSF Membership Overview

## Membership Categories

The organization offers four types:

**Individual Members:**
- "Full Members" – persons active in futures studies for 2+ years with evidence of experience and endorsements from two current Full Members
- "Associates" – those committed to the organization's purpose but lacking sufficient futures studies experience
- "Students" – enrolled full- or part-time at accredited universities

**Institutional Members:**
- "National and international NGOs, academic bodies such as colleges and universities, government agencies, research institutions, business corporations"

## Annual Fees (USD)

Fees vary by country income level (Groups A, B, C):

| Membership Type | Group A | Group B | Group C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full/Associate | $150 | $75 | $30 |
| Student | $50 | $25 | $10 |
| Institutional | $500 | $250 | $100 |

Renewal occurs January 1st annually.

## Key Membership Benefits

Members gain access to:
- Networking with global futures researchers and practitioners
- Private email discussion forum (archived since December 2003)
- Participation in conferences and regional meetings
- "Receive news...about upcoming Futures Events around the globe"
- Member directory and specialist knowledge communities

## Important Note

The application fee is "Non-Refundable."
