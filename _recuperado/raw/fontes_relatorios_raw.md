James Allen Dator (born 1933) is an American futurologist and a former professor and Director of the Hawaii Research Center for Futures Studies in the department of political science and college of social sciences at the University of Hawaii at Manoa. Pioneer futurist Jim Dator has for nearly a half-century sculpted the very discipline of futures studies.
